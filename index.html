FAQ

Before you book

Can you pick us up from Khajuraho railway station or airport?

Yes. Send your train or flight details and we'll be waiting.

Do you arrange hotels as well?

Yes, from budget guesthouses to heritage hotels in every city on your tour.

Are your guides licensed?

Our temple tours are led by experienced local guides who speak English.

Every tour is private and flexible. Add or skip places any time.
